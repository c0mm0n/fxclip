## What's New in fxclip 0.3.0

- 🤖 **fxclip comes to Android** (8.0+): clean links from the share sheet, text-selection menu, quick-settings tile or home-screen widget. Optional Telegram previews adapt X and Instagram links to their destination.
- 🧹 **Clean links first**: the welcome screens and settings now lead with link cleaning, with app previews presented as an optional extra.
- 🐧 **Telegram previews work on KDE** when Telegram uses a generated window identifier. Switching to another app restores the clean original.
- 🛠️ **Android polish**: the tracker counter refreshes immediately, and optional history preserves complete multiline texts when saving and copying them again.
- 📦 **Four downloads, one release**: macOS, Windows, Linux and a signed Android APK. Android builds and tests are now required checks for pull requests.

Android works on demand rather than watching the clipboard in the background. History is off by default; settings can be imported and exported.
