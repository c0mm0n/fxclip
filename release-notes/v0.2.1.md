## What's New in fxclip 0.2.1

- 🐧 **Linux works out of the box on KDE Plasma 6**: the download now includes its own wl-clipboard 2.3, built with KDE's new clipboard protocol (ext-data-control). Nothing to compile, just `./install.sh`
- 🩺 `fxclip check` explains clipboard problems precisely (which tool, which version, which protocol your desktop offers)
